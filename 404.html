What I can certify

Bring the original and I will check it, then attest or certify it where requirements are met.

  • Certified true copies

    Copies of original documents such as certificates, transcripts and deeds.

  • Signature witnessing

    Your signature witnessed in person on the document.

  • Affidavits

    Sworn statements attested for courts, offices and institutions.

  • Statutory declarations

    Formal declarations made and attested for official use.

  • Character certificates

    Certificates for study, work, visas and applications.

  • Identity verification

    Verification of identity for local and overseas requirements.
